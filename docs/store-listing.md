# Microsoft Store listing (Pairly)

## Short description (max 1,000 characters shown above the fold)

Use your Android phone from your Windows PC. Read and reply to texts, see notifications, browse photos and files, mirror and control your phone's screen, and share one clipboard between all your devices. Everything stays on your own network.

## Description

Pairly connects your Android phones to your Windows PC over your home or office Wi-Fi, so you can keep working without reaching for your phone.

Pair once with a short code. After that, Pairly reconnects on its own whenever your phone and PC are on the same network, including after a restart.

What you can do:

- Messages: read your SMS conversations and reply from your keyboard.
- Notifications: see your phone's notifications on your PC and mute the apps you don't care about.
- Photos: browse your phone's photos and videos and save them to your PC.
- Files: send files between your phone and PC in either direction.
- Clipboard: copy on one device, paste on another. Text and images sync to every connected phone.
- Phone screen: mirror your phone in a window and control it with your mouse and keyboard.
- Calls: see who is calling and answer or decline from your PC.
- Multiple phones: connect several Android phones to one PC and switch between them.

Private by design:

- No account, no cloud, no ads, no tracking.
- Your data travels only between your own devices, over an encrypted connection pinned to your PC.
- Each feature asks for its Android permission first, and you can turn any of them off.

Requirements: Windows 10 version 2004 or later, and the free Pairly companion app for Android 8.0 or later (available from the project's GitHub page). Your phone and PC must be on the same network.
